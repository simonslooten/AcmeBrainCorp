CAPITAL INCREASES

 

The Shareholders acknowledge that the Company may need to raise
 
additional funds by way of capital increases. Subject to Article 10 below, any capital increase to be made by the Company will be first offered for subscription by its Shareholders, on a pro-rata basis. If the whole amount of the capital increase is not subscribed by the Shareholders, either as share capital or as loan capital, the remaining capital to be subscribed shall be allocated among the Shareholders willing to subscribe up to the amount requested by each of them and on a pro-rata basis.

 

 

Any Shareholder engaging in activities that could present real or perceived conflict(s) with the Company shall be obligated under this Agreement to disclose such interests to the other Shareholders, in writing upon discovery of said conflict(s).
