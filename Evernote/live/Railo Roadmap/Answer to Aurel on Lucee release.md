# Answer to Aurel on Lucee release

Aurelien,

Good to hear from you again and thanks for your note. 

Yes, we have been aware for some time of the efforts of Rasia and the owners of the GmbH to announce a Railo fork. Not a surprise. What has been interesting, however, is to see the various statements and admissions from the people involved, including from shareholders of TRC. 

 

As you know from your involvement since the beginning with the 4F team, TRC owns all the IP. That does not change in anyway with the recent Lucee announcement. TRC is owned by 3 companies, one of which is 4F in which you hold a stake. As you also know from our discussions and meetings, you agreed to take over the TDAC precisely to ensure the development and release of a commercial version of Railo and, as you said in your various emails, given the strong belief that the “Swiss folks” would not live up to their end of the bargain. You were right and we probably should have listened to you then. In fact, many of your email comments and communications have turned out to be very helpful to our lawyers in our building the case against the GmbH.

 

That said, it is not clear why you would write only to 4F regarding the plans for Railo and not to the othershareholders or to TRC itself. 4F is only one of the shareholders.

It is also unclear why you chose to attend the launch of Lucee, given your obligations as a shareholder in 4F and hence TRC, as well as your prior involvement in the strategy and development of TRC. This may have implications for 4F and for you personally. We have asked our lawyers for advice on this and we suggest you do likewise in France. 

 

Going forward, an email will follow for a 4F shareholders meeting as required to ratify the actions to be taken. Also, since Railo Technologies GmbH still refuses to give us access to our own domains, cms, email server and other systems, we may notify the community, customers and others via a letter to be posted that the IP belongs to TRC and that legal action is being taken.

 

Hope this helps answering your questions. Look forward to your thoughts and to your input at the shareholders meeting.

 

4F Technology Innovation

Simon Slooten

Managing Director
