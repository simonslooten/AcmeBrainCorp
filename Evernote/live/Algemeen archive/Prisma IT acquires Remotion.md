# Prisma IT acquires Remotion

Prisma IT (prisma-it.com) is delighted to announce the acquisition of all activities Remotion (remotion.nl), a Zoetermeer based online software company, with immediate effect. 
“This is the next step in our transition to further strengthen our base and skillset. This acquisition helps us focus even more on what we do best, helping our clients to realize superior digital solutions.
Remotion’s activities will continu under the Prisma IT brand. 
For more information, please contact Prisma IT at 010 - 458 7148 or simon@prisma-it.com.
“Prisma IT is dedicated to helping you realize superior digital solutions”. Founded in 1992 and headquartered in Rotterdam, our down-to-earth approach and commitment to providing clients the right solutions, best technology available and qualified professionals, have made us the partner of choice for organizations of all sizes around the world. Our consultants, trainers and engineers are industry experts in rich media and cloud technologies. We leverage these technologies, our craftsmanship and experience to help our clients realize business critical applications and rich online content delivery solutions across devices.
