# Vacature teksten

PERMANENT POSITIONS

We welcome qualified, dedicated people from all backgrounds who will share our vision, no matter how experienced or new to the business you may be. In exchange, we offer an innovative environment that is conducive to your professional and personal growth and learning, whether in the fields of print media, mobile, online, TV, production, music, or in one of our project management or administrative areas.

Interested? Have a look at the vacancies posted on our job portal, and get in touch with us if you think we might be a good match. We also welcome applications from talented people with skills relevant to our business, even if we haven’t posted any current openings. We look forward to hearing from you!

INTERNSHIPS

Let other companies keep their interns busy with copying and coffee runs. As an intern at Red Bull Media House, you will take on real assignments with real responsibility, and have a chance to make a real impact while you work on building a great CV. It all starts here. Have a look at the intern vacancies on our job portal, and if you feel you’re up to the challenge, we look forward to hearing from you.

 Please do not send us media or data carriers with work samples or showreels if not requested explicitly to do so. If work samples are desired, that will be indicated in the job posting, or we will ask you to send some. Please note that we do not assume any liability in case of any loss or damage.
