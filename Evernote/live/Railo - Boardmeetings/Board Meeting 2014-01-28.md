Re: Support plans - Part 1

Simon, I think that you’re approaching it from a licensed software perspective, and that model is different from a professional open source model.  Plus, I think you and I are thinking about the commercial server and support offerings differently. 

My assumption has been so far that the new pricing arrangement goes like so: 

1. You can use Railo server for free (let’s call it community edition). No support included.

2. You can also purchase Railo deluxe edition (commercial). This costs 200/month per server. No support included. 

3. You can purchase support for either community or deluxe edition. 

4 You can choose which level of support you want, based on the SLA you’re comfortable with. 

5. Organizations with bigger Railo footprints (large number of installs) aren’t eligible for smaller levels of support. Very big footprints must purchase “booster packs” for every additional “N” number of servers over our max limit for Enterprise or Enterprise 24/7 levels. 

I think this model is very clear and easy to understand. But based on your email below, it doesn’t seem to be quite right - can you let me know what I’ve missed?  

I have more to discuss, but I will send in a separate email.

Support plans - 

Simon, thanks for sending over the updated proposal. I still feel that the support contracts are too complicated

Here’s what I propose:

Flat fee for: 

Business
: • £4,999/€5,999/$7,999

Up to 10 servers

Corporate
: • £14,999/€17,999/$23,999

Up to 30 servers

Enterprise
: • £28,999/€34,999/$44,999

Up to 50 servers 

Additional block of 20 servers  - $10,000

Enterprise 24/7
: £49,999/€59,999/$77,999

Up to 50 servers 

Additional blocks of 20 servers  - $10,000

Here’s why this makes sense. 

Under the proposed structures, if you get a client with say, 3 Railo servers, getting a Business Support contract with them is great. But it’s a big hassle if they want to add another server later - what do you charge them? An additional thousand dollars, pro-rated based on the duration of their original contract? And then 3 months later, they want another server - again, more accounting and billing hassle. So it’s a pain for us (the seller) and it’a pain for the client. 

Flat fees are way easier to manage and sell, and I think will end up netting us as much or more money in the end.

Having the additional blocks for the higher levels makes sense, and I think is easy to sell and explain. But not at one-by-one license like is currently proposed. 

Let me know what you think.
