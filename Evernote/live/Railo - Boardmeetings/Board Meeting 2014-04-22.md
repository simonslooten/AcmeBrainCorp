Subject: 
Tomorrow's meeting

Guys,

After last week’s meeting (with a short recap of that tonight), I (and I guess we) have come to the conclusion that this is not going to work.

Again, we only talked about payments, invoices and mistrust. Not one (!!) word was spent on how to generate revenue and how to get TRC to survive.

Railo Gmbh 

As implemented with the Board Resolution of December, TRC will, starting May 1st, issue Purchase Orders for Micha’s work and for Gert’s work.

This PO will be for € 7.5K (or 15 days) per month total.

What I didn’t like about tonight’s meeting is two things:

1. that this proposal was immediately translated into a ‘then only Micha can work, since there are no more hours for the others’. I would like to stress here, that we are all shareholders. At this moment, TRC is able to cover 7.5K per month, that is something completely different (in my view) than 150 hours. It means that 150 hours are paid, but more could be (and should be) done.

2. TRC is not Gmbh’s piggy bank. The main reason for the implemented resolutions was that Gmbh could (and would) take care of their own responsibilities and that they were not (100%) tied to TRC anymore. TRC can (at this moment) simply not be responsible for 100% of Gmbh’s costs, nor should it be. In short, invoices can simply not be based on what Gmbh needs and should even (in my view) be uncoupled from what Gmbh’s people ‘do’.

So, NO, TRC will not issue a seperate PO when the download form needs to be changed.

LGS

As of May 1st, LGS will invoice € 7.5K for their services. Yet they will still do 100% of the agreed work. I would like to add here that LGS, nor their consultants are a shareholder of TRC, yet they are willing to ‘go the extra mile’. They will work at a loss for this account. This will only be implemented as part of an overall deal to ‘save’ TRC.

Website 
&
 Marketing

We should (even without a budget) find a solution for the download form, the marketing message, 
getrailo.org
, the email addresses of non-TRC people and other stuff. During the last 2 years all attempts to get this done, up to my latest email of last week, have died a silent death. It is now time to move on these topics. Can BR help here?

Railo Server

As (yet another) proposal to generate revenues for TRC, I propose to change the license of Railo 5 to GPLv3. Apart from everything else, this would at least give us a tool to talk to customers.

As we all know, this should be done before R5 is released. This proposal is backed by 4F. I will check which percentage of shareholders need to back a proposal like this. If this can be decided by a simple majority vote, I would still like to talk about this (and maybe be convinced to do something else).

Mark

Since Mark has told us that ‘he is out’, we need to change everything that he has access to. All usernames/passwords should be changed to all systems. I had already asked for a list of these systems, but have not received it.
