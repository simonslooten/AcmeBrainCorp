Recap of Railo Board Call today

Simon and Gert, here is a recap of our call today, and my (partial) analysis of the overall state of affairs. We all agree that Railo TRC is technically bankrupt - not enough money to pay current obligations, and not a good enough pipeline of incoming sales to offset current cash flow problems.  There is also a significant problem of mistrust between 4F and Railo GmbH, which is making communication difficult and occasionally contentious.

The situation in 6 months might be considerably brighter if new commercial products are introduced soon, and are commercially successful, but there are no guarantees on that front. Unfortunately, there is currently no clear path to bridge that 6-month period. 

In addition to the overall financial problems, we have an impending crisis; the potential loss of either or both of Gert Franz and Michael Offner.
 This is due to the lack of funds coming into Railo GmbH to pay their salaries. Railo GmbH suffers from both reduced direct revenue (SwissCom is not currently paying for active development), and non-payment of outstanding invoices by Railo TRC. Further complicating things is the fact that Railo GmbH’s invoices to Railo TRC have not been officially approved by Railo TRC, and the amounts owed to Railo GmbH by Railo TRC are not agreed upon by both sides. 

In my opinion, there is no future for the Railo Server product without both Gert and Michael. So any solutions that are proposed and/or acted upon need to solve both the short-term crisis at GmbH (keeping both Michael and Gert employed and focused on the Railo Server product), as well as the long-term financial problem. 

We also have the immediate problem that no LGS activities are taking place, as LGS cannot afford to work without being paid. So Railo TRC currently has no active sales/marketing/accounting resources. This is obviously tremendously detrimental to any ongoing sales efforts, and will have the effect of slowing down an already anemic cash flow situation.

With all of these factors in mind, we have collectively identified several options during our recent discussions: 

===============================================================================

Option 1: Shutting down Railo TRC.
 Railo TRC would declare bankruptcy, and cease operations entirely. No debts would be paid, unless there’s some money left in the bank accounts, in which case some partial payments could be made. In this option, Railo GmbH would have the option to buy back the Railo Server product IP from TRC (but perhaps not the funds to do so).  All shareholders would likely lose their investments.

This would be very damaging to the Railo brand, and incredibly disruptive to any ongoing product development. This is definitely a very difficult path to recommend, although there is a certain clarity to it. I personally want to avoid this, but have the unfortunate sense that it is growing in likelihood. 

TO-DOs for Option 1: None currently.

Option 2: External financing. 
Railo TRC needs a substantial injection of external money (VC or Private Capital). Simon has estimated that TRC needs at least $750K / $1M in order to operate correctly, and to pay bills/vendors, etc. 

Simon (and others in 4F group) have had preliminary discussions with members of Rainmakers forum about this topic, and there was positive feedback about the concept in general. These discussions are *very* early stage, but worthy of pursuit. Timing could be a challenge, as there’s an immediate need for money, and this could be a multi-month process.

TO-DOs for Option 2: Simon to reach out to Rainmakers members and measure real interest, and to report back to TRC Board. 

Option 3: Recapitalization by current shareholders. 
The current shareholders of TRC could inject more capital investment into TRC in order to provide more working capital for ongoing operational costs, salaries, etc. This, if done at sufficient levels, could immediately stabilize the financial picture of TRC for a year or longer. Blue River is willing to participate is this option, but only at a total re-capitalization of the previously mentioned totals of $750K / $1M are met (otherwise we feel we’ll just move into another cashflow crisis). A recapitalization event of $1M would include the following investments from each shareholder based on current equity ratios:  4F $630,000 - GmbH $270,000 - BR $100,000. 

Simon stated that it’s unlikely that 4F would want to participate in this. If 4F did decide to participate, one certain prerequisite for 4F would be to perform a full operational and financial audit of actions taken since founding TRC. GmbH is unlikely to be able to raise money to participate.

TO-DOs for Option 3: None currently. 

Option 4: Blue River to purchase TRC shares from 4F. 
A new idea came up on today’s call - Blue River could purchase some (or all) of 4F’s shares in TRC. While this wouldn’t have an immediate positive effect on cash flow (in fact, it would adversely affect Blue River’s cash flow), it could provide a path to a cleaner operational model, as Railo TRC could be folded into Blue River’s ongoing sales/marketing efforts. 

Simon expressed interest in exploring this option, and it might be one that bears long-term benefits. However, Blue River might need to finance it externally, so it could be challenging from a timing perspective. Plus, it doesn’t help in any with short-term cash problems for Railo TRC. 

TO-DOs for Option 4: Simon to check with members of 4F to measure their interest in this option; if they express real interest, Simon to provide Blue River with proposed cost/percentage of 4F shares. 

Option 5: “Organic Rescue”. 
This option is basically keeping things going under the current arrangements, and waiting for the increased revenues that should come with the commercial products to improve/fix the current financial crisis.  The only way this works at all is if we can find a way to prevent Gert and Michael from leaving - it’s dependent upon quickly closing all current deals in the sales pipeline, and somehow getting enough money into Railo TRC to pay enough to Railo GmbH to pay for Gert’s and Michael’s salaries. Railo GmbH has other obligations, of course, so a focus on just Gert and Michael may be inadequate from Railo GmbH’s perspective. 

TO-DOs for Option 5: None immediate, other than potentially getting Andy Johnson to brief Gert on all open sales discussions for pursuit during LGS “dormant” period. 

Option 6: Writing off LGS and Railo GmbH invoices.
 Simon proposed that in order to “clean TRC’s books” and make TRC more attractive to outside investment, both LGS and Railo GmbH would cancel all outstanding invoices to Railo TRC.  Gert is quite opposed to this; under this scenario, Railo GmbH would cancel invoices of over $100,000 Euro to Railo TRC. This would effectively bankrupt Railo GmbH, and it would be unable to pay current and past due salaries, etc.  

However, there are disputed (or at least not mutually agreed to) amounts at stake here. Both the LGS and GmbH invoices need to be reviewed, itemized and approved or modified before final values can be assigned. 

TO-DOs for Option 6: Review invoices, and finalize exact amounts prior to reviewing this again. 

===========================================================================================

I think this is a pretty comprehensive and accurate summary, but please let me know if there’s anything that's missing or that needs to be modified.
