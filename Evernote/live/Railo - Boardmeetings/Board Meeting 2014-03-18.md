Dear Board Members

In the last few months it has come to the fore that the current relationship between myself and The Railo Company has become untenable and changes have to be made. 

The main issues that have expedited these changes are the lack of communication, clarity and respect. 

With this in mind, I propose the following as the way ahead to “untie the knot”:  

Railo Technologies Limited will be renamed and rebranded. It will be a separate entity not tied to the Railo product or brand. It will have it’s own consultancy and products.

Mark Drew (and RTL2.0 for a better name of the new company) will no longer represent TRC or Railo Server.

Since there is in-house knowledge with regards to Railo Server, a UK partnership agreement can be entered between RTL2.0 and TRC (as is with other companies such a BlueRiver) which would mean that work in the UK can still be carried out and a percentage paid back to TRC.  

A preferential rate for other projects can be discussed based on an hourly or daily rate. 

It has been a long year of many stresses and hopefully this will ease them. 

I shall of course remain an avid supporter of The Railo Company and it’s products as well as a shareholder. 

Regards

Mark Drew

significantly upscale our operation.

As of 1st April I'd like to implement a Partner Board. The purpose will

be to organize our customer facing operation and to manage a consistant

global sales forecast which rolled up to the main Railo board. This will

remove the sales forecasting burden from your weekly board meetings.

The members of this board will be BlueRiver (US), Prisma (Eu), Daemon

(AP) and TRC (AJ). This group will also be useful to serve as a working

party to respond to the market with the required collateral, messaging

and events we need to support the customer facing operation.

enhance the communication within this group, I'd like to create document

repository and an email group
