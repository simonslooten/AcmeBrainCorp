# Railo Mailing Lists

Railo

This group is especially for Railo users, who search solutions for problems concerning Railo and CFML. Post any questions regarding installation, bugs, feature requests, criticism and praise.

Railo Alliance

This is a private group for the incentive of founding the Railo Alliance

Railo Business

This group will mainly focus on the business aspects of Railo. All the contact us emails will go to this group first.

Railo Company

This is the group email for the Railo Company staff members only. If you are in this list, you work for The Railo Company.

Railo Core Developers

This is an internal group for core Railo Company developers working on the engine.

railo-debate

This is reserved for future use as a meta-discussion list for Railo. It will be heavily (entirely?) moderated.

railo-extension-developers

This group is for developers who are building Railo Server Extensions, questions about building extensions are the main focus of this group. If you are looking for general support with Railo Server, head over to
http://groups.google.com/group/railo

Railo Instructors

This group is for instructors teaching Railo

railo-io

This is a private group for the Railo-IO team.

railo-management

This group takes care about the different tasks around Railo business.

 

Railo TDAC

This is the Railo Technical Development Advisory Committee group.

Railo Team

This is the Railo Team group that is restricted to Railo employees and affiliates only.

railo-beta

This group is dedicated to the different Railo Beta projects. It is not for the main product but for products related to Railo like Extensions or Tags 
&
 Functions.

Railo Company

This is the group email for the Railo Company staff members only. If you are in this list, you work for The Railo Company.

railo-contractors

This group is a private group for external contractors to share information about jobs related to Railo Technologies and Railo Consulting.

RailoDocumentation

This group is defunct, join 
http://groups.google.com/group/railo-wiki
 instead.

Railo Partners

Group for companies that are partners of Railo Technologies

railo-prerelease

For discussing pre-release versions of Railo

Railo-Team-Australia

For people in Oz

railo-translate

This group is for the Railo Translation Team to discuss matters relating to the translations of various facets of the Railo open source CFML processing engine, including the installer, the welcome page, and the web-based server administrators.

railo-wiki

Discussion group for those involved in maintaining the Railo wiki.
