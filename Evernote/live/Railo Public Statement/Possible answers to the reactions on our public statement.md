# Possible answers to the reactions on our public statement

1. The reason for a public statement is that a point of no return is approaching, the release of what now seems to be called Lucee 5. Sean Corfield explains the situation perfectly (
http://blog.adamcameron.me/2015/05/questions-for-railo.html#comment-2006280168
). We believe Lucee5 is Railo 5 and was developed by and for TRC. We have not decided under which license this would be released. When LAS releases this code, we can not change the license if we wanted.

2. The reason for the specific way we created our statement is that Gert and the others agreed in a contract not to do exactly what they did, and simply chose not to tell TRC until they had done it. In short, everybody can fork the code, only they can’t because they signed contracts in which they agreed to not do that.

3. One extra remark to answer everyone who read our statement with a little too much creativity: we have not stated anywhere to sue any user of Lucee. They reason we stated what we stated is that organizations might want to be aware of the possibility that they use non-legal code. With current releases of Lucee, a switch to Railo is simple. With upcoming releases that may be less simple.
