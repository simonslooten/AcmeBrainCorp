# ToDo AWS partner network listing

All Standard and Advanced tier APN partners qualify to list their company in the global 
AWS Partner Directory
. 

 

If your firm is currently a Registered tier APN partner and would like to upgrade to the Standard or Advanced tier, please visit the Getting Started section for 
Consulting
 or 
Technology
 APN partners, based on your firm’s business type. 

 

If your firm is currently Standard tier or higher and have not yet published your directory listing, please follow the steps below: 

 

1.   
Log in to the 
APN Portal
*

2.   
Click “Manage Directory Listing” located on the left navigation pane 

3.   
Click “Edit” add your firm’s content

4.   
Scroll down to upload your firm’s logo (please ensure your logo is a .png file and is less than 100KB.  The ideal dimensions are 130x80)

5.   
Click “Save” to submit your firm’s Directory Listing 

 

*Please note that only the designated Alliance Lead from your firm can update your firm’s directory listing. 

 

All directory listing revisions are submitted to the APN Team for review and approval.  During the review period, no edits by the partner can be made. Please allow 3 business days for review.
