# PNL Eindejaars email 2013

Traditionally we organize "year’s end” / review meetings at the end of the year. This year I am planning to change that a little.

The main reason for this is that we  have organized a ‘dual’-feedback (review 
&
 360 degree), accompanied by one on one meetings in July/August/September of this year, which is only a few months ago.

We are planning to do a 360 degree feedback, where everybody will get the opportunity to pick your own reviewers and ask 3 people to fill out
 
your review. You
 will receive invitations for that in the coming weeks.

If you, on top of that, want to schedule an individual chat, please let Andrea know and she will schedule one.

This year has again been a difficult year. We still feel the full effects of the financial crisis. A lot of our customers have big problems finding budgets. Only 2 weeks ago, one of our largest customers, HJL has told us that all development budgets for 2014 have been put on hold. This means that for 2014, we anticipate another difficult year. 

As a result, I am sorry to let you know that there is no room this year for discussions about finance/salaries.

Expanding our markets (by opening new international offices) is one of the scenarios that we follow to ‘escape’ the financial crisis in Holland and to ’spread our risks'. We already see the first positive effects of that with the ‘Mostra’-project that Prisma IT Belgium has won. Development work for this will be done by Prisma IT Netherlands and work is scheduled to begin shortly.

We will try to keep adding new offices if/when/where possible. I am happy to announce that we have just reached an agreement with our friends in Finland. Prisma IT Finland will open in the next few weeks. Juha, Jari 1, Calle and Jari 2 will be our new Finnish colleagues. You will be able to meet them all in the next few weeks, as we are scheduling a visit for them in Rotterdam.

The other thing we can all do is to keep working together and do our utmost to achieve the highest possible quality in our deliverables to our customers. Always take a minute to think if there is anything else that you can do to help our customers.

I am counting on you all to join me in keep working hard to fight this crisis and to come out as winners.

thank you,

Simon
