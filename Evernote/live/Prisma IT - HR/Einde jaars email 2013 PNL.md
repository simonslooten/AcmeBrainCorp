Subject: 
The end of 2013 and the start of 2014

All,

Traditionally we organize "year’s end” / review meetings at the end of the year. This year I am planning to change that a little.

The main reason for this is that we have organized a ‘dual’-feedback (review 
&
 360 degree), accompanied by one-on-one meetings in Q3 of this year, which is only a few months ago.

So for this quarter, we are planning to do a 360 degree feedback, where everybody will get the opportunity to pick their own reviewers and ask 3 people to fill out your review. you will receive invitations for that in the coming weeks.

If you, on top of that, want to schedule an individual chat, please let Andrea know and she will schedule one.

This year has again been a difficult year. We still feel the full effects of the financial crisis. A lot of our customers have big problems finding budgets. Only 2 weeks ago, one of our largest customers, HJL has told us that all development budgets for 2014 have been put on hold. I expect that 2014 will be another year that will not be easy.

As a result, I am sorry to let you know that there is no room this year for discussions about salaries and no room for bonuses.

Additionally, we are also looking to cut costs in the company. We have not planned any high level budget restrictions, but we will be looking at possibilities to cut our expenses. I would like to ask you to help us with that.

Expanding our markets (by opening new international offices) is one of the scenarios that we follow to ‘escape’ the financial crisis in Holland and to ’spread our risks'. We already see the first positive effects of that with projects for the European Commission and NATO that Prisma IT Belgium has won. Development work for the EC project will be done by Prisma IT Netherlands and work has already begun.

We will try to keep adding new offices if/when/where possible. I am happy to announce that we are in the final stages of reaching an agreement with our friends in Finland. The plan is that Prisma IT Finland will open in the next few weeks. Juha, Jari, Calle and Jari ‘Dev' will be our new Finnish colleagues. You may have seen them last week, when they were in Rotterdam.

Although it might not be a simple year, I am very much looking forward to work with all of you to make this a good year. I hope you will help me do our utmost to achieve the highest possible quality in deliverables to our customers. Always take a minute to think if there is anything else that you can do to help our customers.

I am counting on you to join me to work hard to fight this crisis and to come out as winners.

Simon
