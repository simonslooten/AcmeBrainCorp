# PNL changes 2015

All,

A lot has changed over the last few months. In short, we needed to clean up our act. During the last months, we have tried hard to keep people on board and to create a world where all would be happy. But at Prisma IT, just as in the real world, it doesn’t work like that and in the end I realized that we couldn’t go on and I had to “do stuff”. The result of that was that a few people left us. In the slipstream of these changes one or two other people that I really would have hoped to stay, also left our company. 

As always we wish everyone the best of success on their endeavours. 

But we didn’t leave it at that. We have searched for (and found) new people that we really believe will make a difference. Not only in the Netherlands, but also in the other countries we operate in.

Bartolomiej (Bartek) Pietruszewski has joined us as our new Country Manager in Poland.

Juha Kyntäjä has joined us as our new Sales Manager in Finland.

Ronald Bijster has joined us as our new Software Support Engineer in the Netherlands.

Peter Makkes will join us as a Business Intelligence Consultant in the Netherlands on October 1st.

Randy Kana has joined us (temporarily) in Rotterdam to help us startup our NS Narrow-casting screen project.

Frank van den Dungen Bille has joined us (already 6 weeks ago) as our new Sales Executive in Rotterdam.

More new team members will be added shortly.  Stay tuned for more information.

The last (but most certainly not least) announcement in this email is about Ron Pasch. Ron has joined us (for the second time) in December 2004, he originally was employee number 3 (just behind Valentijn, the company dog that we used to have) and has all together been with us for more than 15 years.

After thinking long and hard about it, Ron has decided to make a bold move and start as a freelancer. 

I believe it takes a brave man to do that and I truly hope that he will succeed. Of course from a Prisma IT standpoint we will miss him dearly, 

The good news is that we have agreed that Ron will stay with us as a (part-time) freelance software engineer.
