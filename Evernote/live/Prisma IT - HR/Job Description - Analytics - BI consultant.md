# Job Description - Analytics / BI consultant

Web Analytics Consultant

We are searching for a creative, motivated and talented Web Analytics Consultant to join our Web Analytics team in Rotterdam, Holland. We are looking for someone with strong analysis abilities, intense intellectual curiosity and a self-motivated, proactive mindset. You should enjoy working with both data and people; data analysis and building and maintaining client relationships are two main functions of the job. We will provide all necessary training you need to become an expert in the tools and platforms that we use.

In your daily job, you will typically focus on multiple websites of our clients and support all facets of the decision making platforms and processes (clickstream analysis, outcome analysis, search analysis, multivariate testing analysis). You will also work closely with our technical teams to identify gaps in the data capture strategy and collaboratively implement enhancements. You will be expected to network outside our company to ensure that best practices in metrics and decision making are being exposed to our technical and (web) analytics teams.

Responsibilities

Developing expert level knowledge of Piwik Analytics and other analytics platforms (necessary training provided).

Communicating and transfering your knowledge to clients in terms that are easy to understand and relevant to their business needs.

Delivering and explaining Prisma IT’s (Web) Analytics and Enterprise Dashboard Solutions to our clients.

Consulting with clients as an expert in the use of Piwik and related technologies.

Answering tracking-related questions for clients and internal teams.

Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving existing analytics implementations.

Designing and building custom reports to meet the needs of clients and internal teams.

Performing other duties as assigned.

Typical Deliverables

Weekly, monthly reports (excel, BI tools).

Guide the development of our Alliant Business Intelligence platform (Enterprise Dashboard).

Website behavior and customer experience analysis.

Data consolidation and validation.

Coordinating tags, tracking parameter implementations.

Lead creation and completion of Multivariate and A/B testing documents (from hypothesis creation to influencing creatives to identifying success metrics) and post test analysis.

Business requirements synthesized from multiple sources including product managers, development teams, and functional group members.

Documentation relating to existing processes and suggestion / presentations for improving those processes.

Effective and persuasive presentations (verbal and written) for project teams and business leaders
.

Required Skills

Bachelor's degree in a quantitative or technical field (e.g. Decision Sciences, Math, Statistics, Engineering, Science, etc.)

At least three to four years of working with standard (web) analytics platforms (Piwik, Omniture, WebTrends, CoreMetrics).

Three to five years of business analysis experience in large size organizations with multiple functions/business units preferred.

Excellent communication skills and ability to interact with all levels of end users and technical resources.

Excellent skills to collaborate on creation of project plan and tasks for the team.

Effectively communicate complex ideas to clients and internal team members, both verbally and in writing.

Excellent skills to interpret complex data and analyze results to generate insights.

Solid time management skills.

Desired Skills

Project management skills, business process redesign principles, tools and techniques.

Work effectively both independently and as a member of a cross functional team.

Sound judgment to identify issues and escalates when appropriate.

Contribute to improvements in processes (technical or business) used by analysts.

Drive focused decisions within specific areas and is a key contributor to decisions beyond specific scope of role.

Ability to identify key needs or gaps and provide leadership to close those gaps.

Resolve disagreements and conflicts constructively, knows when to involve others.

Learn from mistakes, take action to apply the learnings and provides peer and team wide feedback for those in immediate area of focus.

Identify and communicate specific personal growth goals for self.

Ability to interpret data and analyze results to generate insights.

Advanced Microsoft Excel skills.

Experience with HTML
