# Letter to StepStone

Dear Pawel, Dear Andrzej,

In early February of this year we had a meeting in Poland that you both attended, together with StepStone's development manager, Bartlomiej Pioro.

During that meeting the plain and simple request to us was: “please give us developers”. The answer to our question: “how many do you need?” was “we can use at least 10 immediately”.

During that same meeting, we also talked about the contract that we needed for this. Your suggestion was to do this under the agreement StepStone currently has with Prisma IT, where the agreed upon rate for development resources is EUR. 570,- per day. This rate was also mentioned in our renewed proposal, sent in December of 2015 and later in the contract drafts.

Prisma IT was able to deliver the first resources shortly after that meeting. Our first engineer started working for StepStone in early february of this year.

In the following month we added another 2 engineers to your teams.

Even more interviews were scheduled with other engineers, although shortly thereafter it was mentioned to us that StepStone really didn’t have need for more than 3 or 4 engineers in total.

In the meantime we sent you a new contract (which was copied from a template that StepStone had provided and that StepStone had used before).

Only after more than 2 months working for StepStone, we received a message that the contract (which was provided by StepStone) needed to be changed because of requirements made by your legal team. After a few revisions of the contract proposal, a new message came that “the prices were not right”.

We have been trying to cooperate with you by sending you a revised price proposal (which under the current agreement we were not required to do).

Our current price proposal is based on:

- Current remuneration levels of highly skilled engineers in Poland

- Costs for Recruitment

- Insurances, Taxes and other additional costs

- The possibility for StepStone to cancel a contract for an individual engineer (example: Tomasz Dopierala)

- An arrangement where Prisma IT covers the “startup costs” for a new engineer provided to StepStone

- A moderate margin

Within our current price proposal, we have enclosed a segmentation, based on skills of engineers and length of contracts. The “target prices” mentioned to me in a recent telephone conversation by StepStone is the “medior" engineer in a ">6 months” contract. 

We currently have classified one of our current engineers working for StepStone as “senior”.

I believe StepStone agrees to that qualification.

In short, we feel that we have sent you a fair proposal.

I would welcome your agreement to the current proposal.

Sincerely,

Prisma IT

Simon Slooten

CEO
