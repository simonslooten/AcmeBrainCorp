✨ Tips

    

        

            
Break big projects into smaller tasks.
  The great thing about a daily to-do list is that you can turn long and complicated projects into more manageable lists!

        

        

            
See all your tasks in one place.
 Visit Tasks in the side bar to see all your tasks in one view.

        

        

            
Constantly reprioritize.
 Did something suddenly become more urgent? Drag and drop tasks within this note so that it's always an accurate representation of your day.

        

        

            
Block off time to get things done.
 Use Evernote Calendar to set up time to work on specific tasks.
