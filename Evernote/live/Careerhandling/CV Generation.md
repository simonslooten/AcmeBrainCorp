# CV Generation

resumegenius.com
cvster.nl
It's due to a number of reasons that all boiuld down to the government not being reliable.
farmers where told to get bigger in the last decades, they had to massively scale up. In order to do that, they closed mortgages for milliions of euros. And now the governement tells them that farming has to get to 50% of what it was. Most farmers stille have huge loans.
we have what we call PAS-melders. farmers need a permission for the amount of manure their animals produce. In some (many) cases, farmers could not get that license and were told to "just carry on and all would be fine". Now their actions are suddenly illegal due to new laws. (the laws are not passed yet).
we have (like all eu countries) so called Natura 2000 areas. in these areas, nature has to be 'stable' or 'improve' (whatever that means). NL chose that they would improve nature by reducing nitrogen deposition. The choice was a Duch one, we could also have chosen to use other mechanisms.
Although our farmers are only responsible for something like 20% of all nitrogen deposition, they were appointed as the 'bad ones'. The governement came up with crazy plans to reduce nitrogen deposition, including a colour map with "problematic areas" (
https://www.veld-post.nl/artikel/480462-overheid-publiceert-stikstofkaarten-per-provincie/
). That map was later debunked as being build on false information. (
https:/
os.nl/collectie/13901/artikel/2433688-stikstofkaart-leidt-tot-onrust-volgens-minister-slechts-vertrekpunt
)
Back to the Nature 2000 areas  .. Holland chose to appoint 162 Natura 2000 areas .. some are not bigger than a soccer field, scattered over the country.
We had a law, that states that states that nitrogen emitting sources should be farther that 25KM away from a Natura 2000 area. In Holland that meant that we could virtually nowhere do things that emit nitrogen (such as building houses).
Last thing is that we chose to use a nitrogen limit of 5 grams per hectare (where Germany has a 700 gram per hectare), effectively closing down the country completely.
Our government chose the farmers as the (almost) single group to solve this problem.
In the last years our fishing fleet has been completely shut down for similar reason, now the farmers fear they are next.
In short .. that's about it!
