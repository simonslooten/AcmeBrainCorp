# Gibson Les Paul Classic Antique HoneyBurst

Martkplaats 17-3-2022 - € 2.250,-
Gibson Les Paul Classic Antique HoneyBurst
Model: LP CAHB NH1
Built: 13/5/2008 in Nashville
Serial: 013480388
Here’s a lovely Gibson Les Paul Classic in a Honey Burst finish, built during 2008.
Built to refer to the 1960 version of the Les Paul Standard, the Les Paul Classic features a slim taper neck and nine-hole weight relief, bringing the guitar to a surprisingly comfortable weight. Aside from the weight relief, the Les Paul Classic follows the usual pattern of a lightly figured Maple cap on a Mahogany back, set Mahogany neck and bound Indian Rosewood fingerboard. The wiring is basic Gibson, individual volume and tone controls and selector switch. 
The Gibson Les Paul Classic Antique features nearly exact specifications that have made the Les Paul the most legendary electric guitar of all time. From the antique body and fingerboard binding to the amber top-hat style knobs and 1960s slim taper neck, it is true in almost every area. You will absolutely love the sound, feel and overall beauty of this fantastic instrument.
Overall, this Gibson Les Paul Classic in honey burst is in good condition. It plays very well!
This guitar has a great look, it’s comfortable to wear and play, it plays cleanly and smoothly, and it’s quite resonant and responsive.  
The black hard shell case is included.
