Time for a quick story :-  The PRS West Street Limited Edition model was originally released in Autumn 2007  as part of a special world wide run of only 300 guitars – However due to  Cites issues with the supply of Brazilian rosewood fingerboards, only a small quantity of the original models ever left the factory – This left PRS with the question about what to do with a quantity of partially built West St guitars  – In the end it was decided to complete the project by offering the remaining models with a Macassar ebony or Indian rosewood fingerboard  – So as I mentioned earlier this is one of the

The West Street Limited Edition guitar pays homage to the original hand built models Paul personally built in the early days in his workshop on West Street, Annapolis, Maryland – Compared to the body shape that we have known for the last 30 years, the early PRS ‘protoypes, along with the Santana model, had a more symmetrical body shape, hence this is the shape retained for the West Street
