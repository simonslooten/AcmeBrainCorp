This Suhr Pro Series S3 is a killer solidbody electric guitar. It is in great shape. No serious issues, dings, dents, wear, etc... Only some slight superficial cosmetic marks if anything, but overall it has been very well cared for. 
The Pro Series S3 from Suhr Guitars is one of those guitars that makes you wonder why you have so many other guitars. It covers so much ground, tonally, that it would suit practically any one looking for a guitar with the classic, Fender Strat-like sparkle of a single coil as well as a bit more oomph out of the bridge pickup. From Blues to Rock, Jazz to Metal- Everything sounds convincing. All while playing easier than any other guitar you've ever played. Seriously! The fit, finish, playability, attention to detail and fretwork of a Suhr Guitar are second to none. Fantastic all around!
