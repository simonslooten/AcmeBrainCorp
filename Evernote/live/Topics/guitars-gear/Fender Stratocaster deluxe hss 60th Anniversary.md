This used 
Fender American Deluxe HSS 60th Anniversary Strat
 is a ridiculously slick rocker that celebrates Fender's 60th and is in Excellent condition. Here with a select Alder body, it has a sweet one-ply beveled gold pickguard, presumably created by Midas himself. It's stocked with a nice HSS configuration so you can get some beefier lows while still maintaining that traditional strat sound with its dual single-coils. At the bridge is the DH-1 humbucker; the middle is a Samarian Cobalt Noiseless; and the neck gets a Hot Samarian Cobalt Noiseless single-coil. Plus it has Fender's killer S-1 switch, so you have even more options among pickup combinations at your disposal.
It has a standard 25 1/2" scale length and a 1.6875" nut width. And rather than a standard synthetic bone nut, it has an LSR Roller nut for reduced friction making for better tuning stability. Other appointments include Abalone dot inlays, Fender's Deluxe two-point Synchronized Tremolo bridge and pop-in tremolo arm, and 22 Medium Jumbo frets along its 9 1/2"-radiused Rosewood fingerboard. Round it all out with a Modern C-shaped Maple neck and you have a real player in your hands. It has minimal playwear and a minor ding at the tip of its headstock (see photos), but plays phenomenally.
