# Untitled Note

Shareholder’s Meeting of The Railo Company, held September 9th, 2014 in London, England at 4:00 PM GMT. 

Attendees:

Malcolm O'Keeffe for Shareholder Blue River Interactive Group, Inc., participating by telephone, representing 10% of the authorized shares of TRC;

Gert Franz of Shareholder Railo Technologies GmbH, participating by telephone, representing 27% of the authorized shares of TRC;

Simon Slooten of 4F Technology Innovation BV, participating by telephone, representing 63% of the authorized shares of TRC (via conference call);

Attendees at the Meeting constitute 100% of the authorized shares in TRC. Participants present constitute a quorum under operative corporate documents. A quorum being present, the Meeting was convened and Simon Slooten was appointed Chairman 
&
 Secretary.

Simon Slooten noted that there are 3 resolutions on the agenda, and requested to discuss other matters prior to voting on the 3 resolutions. Gert Franz and Malcolm O’Keeffe expressed approval of the discussion. 

Simon then stated his agreement as Managing Director with TRC will end “any day now” and the position of Managing Director will then be covered by the Board of Directors. In this new situation, 4F fears that TRC will not be able to do things swiftly enough because the Board of Directors (in its role as Managing Director), will have to decide on everything that TRC should do, and the Board of Directors requires unanimous votes on motions. 4F proposes to appoint Calle Färkkilä  as the new Managing Director for TRC. 

Malcolm O’Keeffe states that Blue River is open to this proposal, but would like time to evaluate the proposal, and have separate discussions, preferably within a couple weeks. Gert Franz states his willingness to consider the matter also (there were no immediate objections to considering the proposal). 

Simon Slooten then stated that 4F proposes to dissolve the Board of Directors. Simon then states he will send an invitation to discuss the matter later in the week. Gert Franz and Malcolm O’Keeffe agree to have the discussion, with Malcolm O’Keeffe clarifying that their agreement is specific to having the discussion, not agreement to the proposed courses of action. 

Simon then asked Malcolm O’Keeffe to clarify his positions on Resolutions 1 and 2, as Simon Slooten believes they are in conflict with each other. Malcolm O’Keeffe then states Blue River’s belief that TRC is a failed company financially and should be closed. Malcolm O’Keeffe then states that Resolution 2 is that if there’s a possibility of moving TRC forward, it would require a thorough examination of the financial records of the company, both its current state and all past transactions. The goal of this would be to eliminate any ambiguity for all shareholders regarding the actual financials of TRC. Malcolm O’Keeffe then responds to a query from Simon Slooten, and states that the first resolution was brought up by Blue River again because, in Blue River’s perspective, the situation hasn’t changed for the better, so Blue River’s opinion on TRC’s viability remains consistent. 

A formal shareholder vote was taken on the first proposed Resolution to dissolve the company. The Resolution failed, with Railo Technologies and Blue River each voting in favor of the Resolution and with 4FTI voting against.

A formal shareholder vote was taken on the second proposed Resolution to create a detailed report outlining all expense and income activity of TRC to date. The Resolution passed, with Railo Technologies, 4Tfi and Blue River each voting in favor of the Resolution.

A formal shareholder vote was taken on the third proposed Resolution to to hire an outside firm to audit TRC’s 2013 tax return. The Resolution failed, with Railo Technologies and Blue River each voting in favor of the Resolution and with 4FTI voting against.

No other Resolutions were submitted in time for consideration by the Shareholders at this General Meeting.

Simon states again that he will send a meeting request to discuss the nomination of Calle Färkkilä as Managing Director. Malcolm O’Keeffe asks that the matters of Resolution 2 (financial report) be added to the agenda for that meeting. Simon states his agreement, and that he will officially notify the Board of Directors of the passing of Resolution 2. 

No date was set for the next General Meeting of the Shareholders, which will be held in accordance with the Company’s rules and statutes.

Having completed all matters duly presented to the Shareholders, the General Meeting was adjourned after an approximately 10 minute duration.
