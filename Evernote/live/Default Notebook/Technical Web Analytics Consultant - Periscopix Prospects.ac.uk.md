Our web analytics team is a rapidly expanding part our business and one of the most established and highly respected in the UK. We partner closely with Google and are currently the largest Google Analytics premium reseller in the UK.

As such we work with a broad range of clients from any sector you could imagine. Our team structure allows you to get hands on experience with so many different datasets that you quickly become adept at understanding a wide range of business and their commercial models. This is a fast paced role that will never leave you sitting around twiddling your thumbs. The learning curve in the first few months is steep but rewarding. The good news is that you will be surrounded by some of the most knowledgeable and talented Google Analytics experts in the Universe. And we run training sessions for a living. As this industry is still young, innovation is constant and rapid. We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager for the bulk of our work but there is a long and constantly growing list of other technologies we employ to get the best results for clients.

Company information
