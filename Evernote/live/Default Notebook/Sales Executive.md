# Sales Executive

Are
 you a successful sales executive? Would you like to help us grow our European customer base? We are searching for a sales professional whose responsibilities will include generating new business in existing markets and in new accounts, as well as playing a key role as you drive sales for strategic, next generation online solutions for organizations.

As a Prisma IT Sales Executive, your job includes

Lead generation

Qualification of leads and sales opportunities

Up-selling and leveraging business from new and established customer relationships

Strategic account planning and execution

Required Skills

Successfully sold into midsize- and large Enterprise companies

Ability to sell to C-Level and across both IT and business units

Strong time management skills

5 - 7 years of solution sales experience selling CMS, Portal technology, Cloud technology, bespoke web applications or similar

A proven track record of closing deals and performing against quota

Bachelor's Degree

Desired Skills

Passion and commitment for customer success

Ability to sell both bespoke applications and platform solutions

Proven consultative sales skills, including the ability to articulate a clear, concise return on investment value statement

Ability to maintain a high level of productivity, manage multiple competing priorities

work effectively under the pressure of time constraints in a fast-paced, complex, collaborative, and team-oriented environment

You will be responsible for driving sales initiatives into different key markets around our own product-, services- and technology stack. Your accounts will be a blend of (inter)national organizations with additional revenue opportunities and prospects.

You must be able to forecast sales activity and revenue achievement while creating satisfied and referenceable customers.

You will manage the complete sales process, which may include the following

Develop and maintain senior-level relationships with people on a managerial level (E-commerce, Information- and IT managers) and executives within target accounts

Diagnose customer’s business issues, latent needs or technology pain points and translate these into a compelling solution and vision

Develop sales and account plans to achieve sales quota;. The focus is on the development of opportunities in new target accounts and development of new markets with our partners

Identify, connect and actively engage with key targets, influencers and decision makers inside an organization

Pro-actively identify new opportunities and qualify generated leads;

Actively funnel opportunities, monitor the pipeline and create and maintain a weekly sales forecast

Drive the development and continuous upgrade of our sales tools and pre sales instruments

Negotiate pricing 
&
 close contracts

The Sales Executive will join our Dutch sales 
&
 marketing team, based in Rotterdam. As a Sales Executive you will report to the Director of Sales Benelux.

The Offer

We realize that we ask a lot. We do this because we like to work with good people. Our people are important to us. Without them we would not be who we are. As with our clients, we aim to start a long term relationship with you. Remuneration and other benefits are above market and we provide a good working environment where work and relaxation are in balance with each other.

We have spacious open workplaces with modern equipment and with all relevant journals and literature at your disposal. Personal development is important to us. There is an annual training budget and we regularly organize 'pizza sessions' (which can also be Chinese, by the way) with our own people and/or people from other companies, to share knowledge. We are open to adapting to new technologies and techniques.

The Procedure

If we have received a resume and a cover letter from you and we like what we read, then we invite you for an initial interview. After that, if you continue on to "the next round", then in your second interview we go into technical and/or organizational depth. Your needs, wishes, expectations and aspirations, as well as ours, are compared and discussed with different people in our organization.

If you come through that and it starts to look like we 'belong with each other', we might ask you to go through a psychological test. After that we decide whether or not to send you a proposal to join us.
