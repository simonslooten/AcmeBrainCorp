# The next chapter

This is the way I see this: I’m searching to anticipate in a group of companies that offer (OS) solutions (preferably based on Java/CFML) that all  have a ‘right to be there’ as an independent company. These companies can offer their solution as OS and (a more feature rich version) as a Closed Source/paid solution.

The added value is that these solutions would all have a way to integrate in a much broader/bigger solution (Railo-Mura-Razuna-dashboards and more). I see this as a Lego kit, whatever you buy, it interfaces (in a superior way) to anything else in the group. Of course the interfaces between the solutions would not be OS.

I see this as a 2 or 3 tier thing. A close connection (part ownership)  of the ‘core-companies’ and a bigger group of ‘friends’ (Bold Alliance) that also are part of the bigger solution (Sven’s video-chat solution for instance).

In this respect I would also be interested in researching the possibilities to buy into Blue River (with 4-friends?).

Tesi’s solutions could certainly be part of this, but because the market they cover, they would probably be a 3rd-tier solution.

I am not aware of anyone doing this. Especially since this is not about creating one huge company, but creating a network of independent companies that together have an integrated solution. The only one I can think of (although they do this based on competences, not solutions is the Cronos group).

If you have time, also have a look at bonitasoft.com. two things there … a nice fit and (what I believe is a) good way to have an OS solutions and more feature rich, paid versions of the same solution.

I agree we should not (at this point) get anyone else (most certainly not Gert) involved.
