# Prompts to optimize Linkedin

1. The Headline Fix
"Rewrite my LinkedIn headline so it sounds like a top 1% operator in [industry]. Make it specific, results-driven, and impossible to scroll past. Give me 10 variations."
2. The About Section Rewrite
"Turn my About section into a story that hooks recruiters in the first 2 lines. Use the PAS framework (Problem, Agitate, Solution). Keep it under 2000 characters and end with a clear CTA."
3. The Experience Glow-Up
"Rewrite each of my job descriptions using the XYZ formula: 'Accomplished X by doing Y, resulting in Z.' Add metrics where possible. Make me sound like I drove real impact, not just 'managed tasks.'"
4. The Skills + Keywords Audit
"Act as a LinkedIn SEO expert. Based on my target role of [role], list the top 25 keywords recruiters search for. Tell me which ones are missing from my profile and where to insert them naturally."
5. The Recruiter Magnet Test
"Roleplay as a senior recruiter at [target company]. Read my profile and tell me — would you reach out? Why or why not? Be brutally honest. List the 5 biggest red flags."
6. The Featured Section Strategy
"Suggest 5 things I should pin to my Featured section to instantly build credibility for [target role]. Tell me exactly what each should say and why it works."
7. The Outreach DM
"Write me 3 cold DM templates I can send to recruiters at [dream companies]. Make them short, personal, and impossible to ignore. No corporate fluff."
