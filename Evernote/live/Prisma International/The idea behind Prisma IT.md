# The idea behind Prisma IT

Vision: Uncomplicate life through better, Down-to-Earth IT solutions

Mission

Moto

Gents,

I realise that my mission in the coming weeks is to share with you the way I have been thinking of Prisma IT (the brand) for the last 23 years and how we use this going forward. Although it doesn't look that way, I'd like to believe that there always has been some coherence in our messaging and behaviour. We need to discuss this more in depth. My messaging has always been:

I 'just' want to have fun, so humour, tong-in-cheek and 'making fun of ourselves', within limits of course, is important

What we do is no rocket science

Our approach is 'down-to-earth' (there is a reason why I don't wear suits and ties at work). High quality, but down to earth.

I hate pay-offs, hence the 'we do stuff' payoff

We have the best people in the industry (yes we are a 'tad' arrogant)

I was a huge fan of Apple's 'think different' campaign: think outside the box, think really good about the message (
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yefGOif2WU
)

Making references to movies is important. (like Morpheus being the one that brings you back to reality and shows you what is really going on in the world)

our focus is very narrow. We only do a few things, but we do them 
really
 good. We help to build software on the Java platform. Our aim is broad (horizontal), our focus is narrow.

In a more philosophic approach: the prisma in the logo stands for converging individual components into one coherent solution ('a beam of white light') . That is why the logo is mirrored from all ether (same) pics that you know.

Our mision: do fun projects with interesting customers and deliver the best solutions with the best people in the business.

Our vission:  
Prisma IT is dedicated to helping you realize superior digital solutions

So, I guess I have been trying to walk that fine line of being 'different' and still being taken (very) seriously by big companies and organisations
