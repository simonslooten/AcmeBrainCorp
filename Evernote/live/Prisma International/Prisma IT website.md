Platforms

For almost 25 years we have been developing software systems. Since ’94 we have been focussing on the internet. That makes us a dinosaur in our field of work. Our experience with different development platforms and programming languages has given us tha advantage of being able to pick the ones that we like most. The ones that we feel are the best for the solutions that we create for and with our customers.

CFML

The ‘Grande Dame’ of web development platforms. One of the first ones that were released when the web emerged. And stil around today. Stronger than ever, better than ever.

But … who uses it, you may ask. Well, NASA, European Commission, NATO, Boeing are just a few of the big ones. Fandalism is one of the hip ones. A nice article about CFML can be found here: 
http://java.dzone.com/articles/6-reasons-why-our-start
.

We focus on Railo and on Adobe ColdFusion.

Java

As you may know, CFML runs on the JVM. We use Java in parts of our application where it makes sense to use Java. In short, we use CFML where we believe it makes sense and we use Java in areas where CFML ‘doesn’t fit’.

The result are very stable, well performing and scalable applications that are very affordable to develop.

Flex

Another langua that has been around form any years. Why do we still use it? The applications that we design and develop are for more than 90% business critical applications that will be used for many years by our customers. We need to make sure that apps that we built are supported, from the UI to the Data layer. That makes us a bit picky on which platforms we use. We tend to stay away from ‘the flavour of the week’ platforms. You never know if they will be around next month.

And so, the applications that we built in the early years of the 21st century are still in use by our customers and thus are still maintained by us.

LiveCycle

One of our specialities. we have been working a lot with LiveCycle, and we still do. Poorly neglected by the management of the company that owns the technology, but still very much cared for by the people that run the business unit. A Business Process Management system with very strong workflow and pdf management capabilities, in use by many large international organizations. Our vast knowledge of LIveCycle even made our consultants co-writers and co-editors of the official Adobe LiveCycle Training Curriculum.

Prisma International

After almost 23 years of being a stable technology company, we felt the time was right for a new challenge. Our international focus has always brought us to different places and countries and has given us the privilege to work for many international organizations around the globe. So, a few years ago, we have taken the decision to expand into different countries. We started with opening offices in 4 additional countries, France, Belgium, Great Britain and Poland. Shortly after, we opened offices in Finland and 3 offices in Germany (Munich, Hannover and Berlin). We are already working on a new set of countries that we would love to add to our list.

Apart from new offices, we have also been very lucky to be able to add great specialised teams of people to our organization. Recently, one of the best mobile app building teams, the wonderful guys from 2CoolMonkeys have joined us. A while ago the very skilled consultants of Clickview have joined to strengthen our offerings and skill set in the Business Intelligence business unit of Prisma IT.

The way we operate our expansion makes the organization stronger from the day we open a new office or add a new team in a country we are already in.

So today, we are able to offer your what we have always been offering you, but with an extra added value. We are able to operate locally from more locations, with more specialised people. This approach strengthens our mission, to help you realize the best online solutions. 

Prisma IT Training

We have been focusing on training from the very early days. We are passionate about what we do and how we do it. We love to transfer our knowledge of the platforms and systems we use to your engineers and consultant. Our approach in training might be a little ‘off the beaten track’. We are not a big training organization and we don’t have dedicated trainers. And there is a reason for that. We believe training should be delivered ‘from the field’, by certified specialists who spent most of their times in ‘real projects’ with ‘real customers’. So our trainers start as consultants, working on customer projects. Only when they have a vast knowledge of a platform or product, they can apply to become a certified trainer.

That approach enables us to guarantee you that our trainers will have ‘real life’ experience and that they transfer their knowledge of real projects.

We keep the group size small, we want to be sure to be able to give your people all the time needed to get the most value out of a training.

Our training offerings range from public training in our own training rooms to tailor made, specialised training in your offices or online. Please contact our sales people with all the questions you may have on training.

Prisma International

After more than 23 years, we decided to start a new chapter.In the last 23 years we have worked all over the world, were lucky enough to work for customers that we are still very proud of, had the privilege to partner with companies that we feel were (and still are) instrumental to what the web has become today and we have partnered with companies that have proven to be utterly unreliable. In short we’ve been there, we’ve done it and we have all the t-shirts.

And now, since the last quarter of last year, we felt we needed something new.

It was May 2013, in a hotel bar in Warsaw. A few of us said "why don’t we go international?” And so we did.

Today, Prisma IT is present in 8 countries, with offices (in most of them), local people (that know the country, the language and the culture). We have the privilege to work with what we believe are the best people in our business in each country. You may know some (or maybe al)l of them, some are well known in our part of the universe. We are proud of them and we are proud that they are now part of our family. 

Prisma IT is now a European brand but still, we work just how you know us, locally. We are having fun, we work hard and we try to be better. Not a lot, just a little bit. A bit more down to earth and a little bit better.

Come and talk with us, we are now close to you, in Rotterdam, Paris, London, Brussels, Warsaw, Helsinki, Stockholm and Frankfurt.

O, and stay tuned, we’re not done yet.
