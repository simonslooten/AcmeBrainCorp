# My philosophy

I realise that my mission in the coming weeks is to share with you the way I have been thinking of Prisma IT (the brand) for the last 23 years and how we use this going forward. Although it doesn't look that way, I'd like to believe that there has been some coherence in our messaging and behaviour. We need to discuss this more in depth (probably in a separate session). My messaging has always been:

I 'just' want to have fun, sp humour, tong-in-cheek and 'making fun of ourselves' (I don't know the english word), within limits of course, is important

It is no rocket science, it is a digital rolodeks, nothing more

Our approach is 'down-to-earth' (there is a reason why I don't wear suits and ties at work)

I am not a fan of pay-offs, hence the 'we do stuff' payoff

We have the best people in the industry (yes we are a 'tad' arrogant)

I was a huge fan of Apple's 'think different' campaign: think outside the box, think really good about the message (
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yefGOif2WU
)

I love making references to movies. (like Morpheus being the one that brings you back to reality and shows you what is really going on in the world)

In a more philosophic approach: the prisma stands for diverging 'problems' into smaller components that we solve and/or converging individual components into one coherent solution ('a beam of white light') .

Our mision: do fun projects with interesting customers and deliver the best solutions with the best people in the business.

Our vission:  
Prisma IT is dedicated to helping you realize superior digital solutions

So, I guess I have been trying to walk that fine line of being 'different' and still being taken (very) seriously by big companies and organisations.
