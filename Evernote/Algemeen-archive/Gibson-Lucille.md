Gitaar is eigendom van een verzamelaar. Heeft altijd in de koffer gezeten. Deze gitaar is nieuw! 

  

  

Hieronder de originele aankondiging voor deze Limited B.B. King Lucille. 

  

  

Custom Shop Announces Limited Edition B. B. King ES-355 06/01/2006 Gibson Custom is proud to announce the introduction of a new King of the Blues Lucille guitar honoring the great B.B. King. 

  

  

Born Riley B. King, B.B. is generally recognized as the leader of modern blues. A native of Indianola, Mississippi, the former cotton picker moved to Memphis in the 1940s where he honed his guitar chops while also working as a disc jockey for radio station WDIA. He gained national prominence in the early 1950s with a string of hits beginning with the R&B smash, ìThree OíClock Blues.î Since Kingís arrival on the music scene, virtually every electric guitarist who has held an instrument in their hands has been influenced by him directly or indirectly. In 1987, King was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame and received a Lifetime Achievement award from the National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences. King has also been recognized for his efforts in preserving and promoting Americaís traditional arts, receiving the National Medal of Arts in 1990 and Kennedy Center Honors in 1995. In 2005, a handful of the artists who have been inspired by King appeared on his critically acclaimed album, 80. Eric Clapton, Van Morrison, Billy Gibbons, Roger Daltry, and Mark Knopfler were just a few of the artists who paid tribute to King. 

  

  

This stunning new Custom Shop Lucille features gold 490R and 490T Humbucker pickups, pearl block inlay on the fingerboard, and an engraved pickguard with the ìKing of the Bluesî logo. 

Each King of the Blues Lucille will carry an engraved pearl plate with the production number. 

A certificate of authenticity signed by King himself will be issued with each instrument. 

  

  

Features: 

  * ES-335-style semi-hollow body Arched laminated maple top

  * Laminated maple back and sides No f-holes

  * Multi-ply top and back binding

  * Set three-piece maple neck 22-fret bound ebony fingerboard with large pearl block inlays

  * Multi-ply bound headstock with black overlay,

  * Gibson logo, gold truss rod cover, and pearl plaque on back of headstock (reflecting the number of the 150)

  * Nashville tune-o-matic bridge TP-6 tunable stop tailpiece

  * Raised non-bound pickguard with hand drawn "Crown" and "King of Blues" logo inscription

  * Stereo electronics

  * Four amber knobs with silver inserts (two v, two t)

  * Vari-Tone rotary tone knob

  * Three-way pickup switch

  * Two 1/4 in. outputs

  * Gold hardware 24.75 in. scale

  * Black levant 'Gibson/ B.B. King' case

  * B.B. King signed Certificate of Authenticity

Andere unieke gitaren in voorraad: Pensa Custom, Pensa-Suhr, Suhr, Schecter, Tom Anderson, PRS, Gibsn, Fender.
