We then combine that data with other data from your organization, from SalesForce to SAP, from Big Data to Big-Iron, we can handle all.  
Our engineers work with you to add more knowledge and meaning by identifying, naming and tagging fields and data points.  
  
**Analysis**

We can feed your in-house monitor systems with the analysed data. We can send notifications via email, RSS, twitter, SNMP or any other feed. We can generate reports in PDF, Excel or any file output format that you may need to further use that data in other systems

**Dashboards**

Alliant Dashboards are offered as a SAAS solution or on premise. 

Alliant dashboards cover 4 strategic areas:

  

**Operational Intelligence**

These dashboards are used by departments and its employees. Operational dashboards provide detailed insights, while general reviews aren't a part of their users’ interest. They have to be prepared for enabling detailed analysis and particular processes. They give insights in running processes.

  

_Customers_ _: MinAZ, RaboBank, Crisis.nl_

  

**Tactical Intelligence**

Tactical dashboards are prepared for more detailed purposes, usually being used for tracing the trends in relation to company's goals and initiatives.  
With these dashboards, managers can control how market reacts on company's actions and they can investigate how each particular task influences on the company as a whole. Tactical dashboard are usually measured against the preset goal.  
Tactical dashboards allow deeper insights into analyzed data, discover the problem - and trace its history, find why it had occurred, and – basing on gathered information – make well-thought-out decisions which lead to improving the current situation.  
  
_Customers_ _: Juridisch Loket, PB51, ICCO, Oxfam Novib_  
  

**Strategic Intelligence**   
Strategic dashboards are connected directly with the balanced scorecard methodology (David Norton and Robert Kaplan).  
They are used for monitoring the company's progress in achieving predefined goals, defined through KPI's.  
Strategic dashboards are used on the highest level of company's hierarchy. They provide a ‘ oom’ functionality to allow management to "dive down" to lower levels in the hierarchy. They allow executives to see the generalities first, and then drill through to more detailed reports.  
  

_Customers_ _: RaboBank, Overheid.nl, Randstad_
