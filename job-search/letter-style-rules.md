## Interest mails (recruiter, aim = call) — from SL-28 SENT, 8 Oct 2026
Source: Simon's own rewrite of our SL-28 draft, sent 8 Oct ~11:33 to Top of Minds (`SL-28/Mail_TopOfMinds_SL28_SENT_2026-10-08.md`; analysis `SL-28/SL-28-post-send-analysis-2026-10-08.md`). These rules apply to short recruiter mails that aim at a call. Formal RvT/RvC letters keep the rules above.
1. **Length and shape.** Aim for about 220–290 words of body: five or six short paragraphs plus a one-line closer. The old 120–180 cap was too tight (SL-28 sent: 283). Put the extra words into method and vacancy fit, never into adjectives.
2. **Hook = their words, then own it.** Quote the CEO's or vacancy's key line verbatim, in its original language (English is fine), inline after a colon. Follow it straight away with a first-person ownership line ("Die invalshoek is me op het lijf geschreven."), not a soft "ik herken me".
3. **Show the angle before the CV.** One paragraph reframes the client's problem the way Simon would ("niet welk systeem eruit moet, maar welke klantstap nog bestaat omdat niemand hem heeft durven schrappen"). Method comes before résumé, and CV receipts come after.
4. **Mirror the vacancy's criteria as a position, in Dutch.** Use their own yardsticks (e.g. "snelheid, klantwaarde, operationele impact en vooral gemak voor de klant"; "AI als operationele laag, niet als pilot ernaast"). This is allowed when it is stated as approach, never as an experience claim (no ERP-replacement or sector claim that the CV lacks).
5. **Receipts without shrinking.** Say "mijn bedrijven" rather than naming small own companies, and lead with the biggest true frame (Prisma IT 1992–2019, zes landen, internationale organisaties en overheden). Never add a line that diminishes scale.
6. **One positive fit line on logistics.** Keep it terse and confident ("Zes tot negen maanden, 32 uur, Oost-Nederland: dat past."). Never frame it as a limitation or hedge.
7. **Close on the real criterion.** Name what decides the outcome, plainly ("Of dit werkt, hangt af van de klik met de CEO."). Then ask for a short call, write "Mijn cv heb ik bijgevoegd." and end with "Ik zie uit naar jullie reactie." No "hopelijk".
8. **Register and form.** "Beste [voornaam]," with je/jullie (no u). The subject is "[Rol] (ref. [nummer]) – Simon Slooten". Colons for punch are fine in mails. Sign off "Met vriendelijke groet, / Simon Slooten"; the contact line is optional (he dropped it on SL-28, probably because of his mail signature, which is unverified).

**Undercurrent check** (see the TOV-REFERENCE "Undercurrent" section; this is my reading of SL-28, not Simon's words): the rewrite already carries it without naming anything.
- Plain basic question, the Fulghum strand: "Eerst de aannames eruit".

Keep weaving it in that way, as tone only and never as quotes or names.
