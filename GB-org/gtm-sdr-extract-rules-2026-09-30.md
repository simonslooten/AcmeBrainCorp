## The 3 rules

### 1. CLEAR vs FLAG intake (SDR)
Every contact/account in an outbound or BD batch lands in exactly one bucket:
- **CLEAR** — safe to draft and *stage unsent* (no active conflict, no duplicate path, signal present).
- **FLAG** — edge case; human (Simon / BD) decides before anything moves.

No silent middle. FLAG stops the machine; CLEAR keeps moving to stage-only.

### 2. Stage, don’t send (SDR + standing Simon send-gate)
Bots may research, draft, create records, and enroll **unsent**. Humans KEEP and send. LinkedIn / mail / LinkedIn connection notes never auto-send. Matches Bonica + existing park-for-Simon.

### 3. Signal-first packets → morning review (GTM + SDR)
- First touch opens on a **real account signal** (prior conversation, public statement, footprint/usage, recorded ask) — not a generic template or title-only cold.
- Overnight research/drafts land as a **morning review list** (KEEP / FLAG / skip), not an empty chat. Prefer one CoS/BD packet over five parallel pings.
