## What the post says (steal)
Stop prompting “make it sound human / add natural mistakes.”
AI does not know “human.” Define concrete behaviors instead:

1. Contractions + mixed sentence length (short punch, long breath)
2. Tangents + show thinking (not polished essay from sentence 1)
3. Conversational, simple language
4. Texture via imperfect structure (not every paragraph same shape)
5. Opinion, edge, specific examples

## Map onto Simon (already locked ToV — do not invent a new persona)
Use these as **promptable rules**, not vibes:

| EXM tip | Simon version |
|---|---|
| Contractions + length mix | Spoken Dutch/English; short lines that land; then one longer breath. Never headline fragments. |
| Tangents / thinking | Show the turn: “Dat klinkt als X. Het is Y.” 10th-man beat OK. One metaphor max if it earns place (Gallic village / Don Quixote only when topic is lock-in). |
