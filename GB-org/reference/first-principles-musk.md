# First principles thinking (Elon Musk): reference
Saved 8 Oct 2026 at Simon's request ("we gaan dit nodig hebben"). Use it in letters, articles, interviews and the CEO conversation for SL-28.

## Core idea
Most people reason by analogy: we do something because that's how it was always done, or because others do it, with small variations on a theme. First principles thinking breaks a problem down to the facts you are truly sure of, and reasons back up from there. It takes more mental energy, but it is how you get to genuinely new solutions.

## Quote (verbatim, Kevin Rose "Foundation", Sep 2012)
"I think it's important to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. The normal way we conduct our lives is we reason by analogy. We are doing this because it's like something else that was done, or it is like what other people are doing, slight iterations on a theme. First principles is a physics way of looking at the world, and what that really means is that you boil things down to the most fundamental truths and then reason up from there. That takes a lot more mental energy."

## Battery example
- Analogy: "battery packs cost $600 per kWh and always will, because they always have."
- First principles: what is a battery made of (cobalt, nickel, aluminium, carbon, polymers for separation, a steel can), and what do those materials cost on the London Metal Exchange? About $80 per kWh.
- Conclusion: the materials are not the problem. The opportunity lies in combining them cleverly into a battery cell.
- Later fact (Startup Archive): in Nov 2023, battery packs reached a record low of $139/kWh, about $103 in 2012 dollars.

## Dutch summary (for use by Simon)
Musk legde het in 2012 zo uit in een interview met Kevin Rose. De meeste mensen redeneren via analogie: we doen iets omdat het zo altijd ging, of omdat anderen het ook zo doen. Bij first principles breek je een probleem af tot de feiten waar je echt zeker van bent, en daarvandaan bouw je weer op. Het bekendste voorbeeld zijn batterijen. Iedereen zei dat ze $600 per kWh kosten en dat dat zo zou blijven. Musk rekende uit wat de grondstoffen kosten, ongeveer $80 per kWh. Het probleem zat dus niet in de materialen, maar in hoe je ze combineert.
