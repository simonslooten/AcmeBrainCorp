## 2. What to change in craft

### Hooks (line 1)
- **Do:** Negation or board-room stake in ≤12 words. (“The problem is not that AI is evil.” / “We may just be wood.”)
- **Don’t:** News lede, movie/band insider, series callback, scene-setting without tension.
- Test: *Would a stranger NL mid-market CIO finish line 1 without knowing you?*

### First 2 lines
- Line 1 = claim. Line 2 = concrete noun or ladder step (weed / ant / granny / board FOMO).  
- Move “last week / part of a series” to **footer only** (Embodiment’s link block is the model).

### Cards
- Card text = the **shareable sentence**, identical to the thesis (not a softer paraphrase).  
- Keep navy/mesh + gold “CLARITY IN THE AI NOISE” / AcmeBrainCorp — brand recognition is forming.  
- Prefer one hard line over poetic drift (“Heads…” vs “Hands…” — pick the verb that matches body).

### CTAs
- Replace “stuur me een bericht / contact me” as the **only** ask.  
- End with **one opinion question** a peer can answer in 1–2 sentences (e.g. “Where is ‘in the way’ already true in your stack?”).  
- Brand line stays; DM ask can sit in first comment, not body.

### Length
- Keep 350–550 for essays, but **land the sticky sentence by ~40% of the read**, then prove, then rest.  
- Alternate: one short punch (120–180w + card) every 2nd post without breaking series logic.

### NL / EN
- **One language per post.** EN for Survive/Merge thesis posts that should travel; NL for Kids/school HOLD and local board pieces — full NL, no EN hooks.  
- No mid-post code-switch, no song titles as hooks unless the claim stands alone without them.

### Series glue
- Number lightly in footer: “1 Moral grammar · 2 Wood · 3 Embodiment · next: Survive.”  
- Body must stand alone. Recap prior claim in **one independent sentence**, not a hyperlink dependency.
