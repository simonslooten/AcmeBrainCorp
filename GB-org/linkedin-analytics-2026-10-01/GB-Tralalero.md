# Tralalero craft take — LinkedIn Discovery vs engagement
1 Oct 2026 · for CoS · ≤25 lines · no Brainrot names · no Simon ping

**Read of the numbers:** Discovery is up (+1644% / 28d). Best out-net = moral-radius / “AI is not evil” (B: 323, 48% out). Worst out-net = Near misses (D: 90% in). Optimus (A) too young to judge. Engagement-low is Simon’s call — dumps have no reactions/comments, so this take is craft, not vanity-metric theatre.

1. **Hooks.** Lead with the turn, not the setup. B wins because line 1 denies the usual frame (“not evil”). Soft scene-setting (“Until now AI has mostly lived in computers”) is true but cold — save scene for line 3+.
2. **First two lines.** Must carry one claim a stranger can steal. Pattern: short punch → one longer breath that names the risk (harvest / wood / body≠conscience). Cut throat-clearing.
3. **Cards.** One concrete sentence, not a slogan. Winners: “we may be wood” / “Heads do not install a conscience.” Losers: abstract board-speak. Card = the claim you could shout across a table.
4. **CTA.** One specific question tied to the claim (“Where do you still treat people as terrain?”), not “curious what you think.” Ask for a story or a disagreement — that is how comments start. Still agency before lock-in: invite judgment, not FOMO.
5. **Length.** Stay 350–450 public words when the goal is reply, not treatise. Spine posts can run longer; comment bait should not. Cut the third example.
6. **NL / EN.** One language per post. Mixed NL+EN (Near misses) kept reach warm/in-network. Kids/AI school stays NL as locked; Survive/Merge EN unless Simon says otherwise. Mixed feed OK; mixed *inside* one post costs out-net.
7. **Voice keep.** Truth over theatre. Survive = diagnose, Merge = agency. No tip-swarm, no join-AI-now, no product pitch in thought posts. Cards and CTAs sharpen the same claim — they do not sell a seat.
