## What Simon changed vs our v2 (the learnable deltas)
1. **No headings, no separators.** One continuous run of short paragraphs. "What 'further' means", "Three receipts" headers, `---` lines: gone. A paragraph opener carries the turn ("Three receipts." as its own line is fine).
2. **Softer, defensible claim.** "Further than 99%" became "Further than almost anything I see in the wild". No numbers he can't back.
3. **No filler connectors.** Dropped "To be clear:", "And", "So", "Now", "This is the part I'd look for if I were buying."
4. **Sentences over punctuation tricks.** Parentheses and colon-lists became full sentences ("The hire bar, which is what a bot must prove…"). No arrows (→) and no slashes ("steal/skip" became "steal-or-skip", "link → steal/skip" became "Link, steal or skip").
5. **Less internal machinery.** "my Chief of Staff bot" became "the Chief of Staff"; "a separate quality bot" became "a separate quality check, by someone who did not write it"; "checked by QA" became "checked"; "eight-step self-critique" became "the same pass" (the steps are still listed, but not branded).
6. **Neutral tools.** "ChatGPT" became "a model". No brand names unless they're the point. The one exception, for Grok Bot, is defined below under "Scoped exception".
7. **"Here", not "In our org".** First person and plain.
8. **CTA:** "send me 'steal'" (not "DM me"). Short, no colon setup.
9. **Sign-off:** tagline + brand on their own lines: "Clarity in the AI noise." / "AcmeBrainCorp".

## Constants he kept (keep doing these)
- Short declaratives, one idea per sentence. Dry punchlines ("Speculation is not an invoice." "None of this is clever. It is just enforced, every time.").
- Receipts over claims: concrete examples, each with what we did and what we skipped.
- Honesty markers: "my read as an operator, not a survey".
- Human-in-the-loop framed as strength: "Keeping me in on purpose is not a limitation."
- Plain English, British spelling allowed ("systemise", "labelled").

## Do not
Headings in LinkedIn long-form · stats or % he can't prove · tool/brand name-dropping (one scoped exception, see below) · arrows/slashes/parentheses as shorthand · internal bot names or jargon (Bonica, tape-as-term without explanation, Mission Contract) · party names.
