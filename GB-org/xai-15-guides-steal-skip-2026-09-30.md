## GTM (what it is)

Go-to-market: how an enterprise AE/GTM lead runs bots around selling. Author: Krista Letz (SpaceXAI). Stack: CoS (meeting prep, inbox, post-call, orchestration) · overnight prospecting bot · one customer-expert per strategic account (weekly media rundown prompt) · data / product expert · forecasting · Figma slides (incl. live update from call transcript) · sales coach on Gong. Connectors first (CRM, mail, calendar, Slack, notes). Steal for us = prompt patterns + CoS orchestration habits, not cloning the fleet.

## SDR (what it is)

Sales development (outbound before AE). Author: Stefan Markarian (SpaceXAI). Five steps: Research → Prospect → Draft → Intake (CLEAR vs FLAG) → Finalize (human KEEP/send). Cursor builds research + first-touch; Grok Bot stages CRM/sequences/LinkedIn when laptop closed. Hard rules: never auto-send; warm history beats cold template; footprint beats title; blank LinkedIn requests; repeated feedback → skill. Steal for us = CLEAR/FLAG + stage-don’t-send (already Bonica-aligned).
