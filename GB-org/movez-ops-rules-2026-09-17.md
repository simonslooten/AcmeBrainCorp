# Movez standing ops rules — 2026-09-17

Tags: `#onepager #steal-skip #archive #movez`

Source: Simon yes after CoS analysis of [@0xmovez 10-steps](https://x.com/0xmovez/status/2100261918896169259). Locked into CoS profile, [Material output bar](sand-workflow:material-output-bar), Lirili Cost hygiene weekly, Lirili agent description.

## 1. Attention list
CoS maintains a **live filter** from what Simon actually opened, answered, or sat in (mail / calendar / chat / bridge) — **not** Monday intentions. Other bots check work against that filter. Divergence between written priorities and real attention is a **finding** to surface, not something to paper over.

## 2. Stale-data policy
If a source is missing, empty, unreachable, or older than the decision needs: **report that and stop**. Never invent a confident fill. Gaps go on the tape block under Skipped / Guesses.

## 3. Rung-6 (routines)
Create or tighten a routine **only after** retries and failure cases are defined. A schedule on an unproven process just fails on a timer.

## Owners
- CoS: holds attention list; enforces all three on routing and new routines.
- Lirili: audits all three on Friday Cost hygiene weekly; reports to CoS only.
- Specialists: stale-data via Material output bar tape; no new routines without rung-6 via CoS.
