> **The FOMO is strong with this one**
>
> We’ve seen this before.
>
> Dotcom. Web 2.0. Crypto. Every major technology wave creates its own FOMO cycle. Boards get nervous, competitors make announcements, and suddenly everyone inside the organisation starts asking: “What can we do with this?”
