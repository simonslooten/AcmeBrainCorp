**What is a Fractional Chief AI Officer?**  
A Fractional Chief AI Officer (fCAIO) provides executive-level AI leadership on a part-time or retainer basis. You gain access to senior strategic expertise, governance oversight, and implementation guidance — tailored to your organisation’s size, maturity, and goals.

This model is especially effective for Dutch companies that need clear AI direction but do not yet require (or cannot yet justify) a full-time Chief AI Officer.

**Why It Matters Now**  
Many organisations in the Netherlands are investing in AI but lack dedicated leadership to ensure these investments deliver real value. At the same time, the EU AI Act is increasing the need for proper governance and risk management.

A Fractional CAIO helps you:
- Define a clear and prioritised AI strategy
- Avoid costly missteps and vendor-driven decisions
- Build the right governance and compliance foundation
- Accelerate responsible AI adoption with measurable outcomes

**Key Benefits (4 cards)**
1. Cost-Effective Expertise — Access senior AI leadership at a fraction of the cost of a full-time Chief AI Officer.
2. Flexible Engagement — Scale support up or down depending on your needs — from a few days per month to project-based work.
3. Independent & Objective — Vendor-agnostic advice focused on what is best for your organisation, not on selling technology.
4. Faster, Better Results — Clear strategy, strong governance, and practical implementation support that accelerates value realisation.

**What’s Typically Included**
- AI strategy development and prioritised roadmap
- Governance framework and risk assessment
- EU AI Act readiness review and compliance support
- Independent evaluation of use cases and technology choices
- Oversight of agentic AI and automation initiatives
- Board and leadership reporting
- Team upskilling and capability building

**Who It’s For**
- Supervisory Boards and Non-Executive Directors (RvC / RvT)
- CEOs and leadership teams of scale-ups and mid-sized companies
- Professional services firms
- Public sector and government organisations

Organisations that want strategic clarity and responsible AI adoption without the overhead of a full-time executive hire.

**Engagement Models**
- Retainer model — A set number of days per month for ongoing strategy and oversight
- Project-based — Focused engagements such as AI strategy development or governance design
- Hybrid — A combination of regular advisory and project work

**Our Approach**  
We combine deep executive experience with a modern, agentic AI operating model. This allows us to deliver high-quality, independent advice efficiently and at scale.

Our focus is always on **clarity, pragmatism, and measurable outcomes** — not on technology for its own sake.
