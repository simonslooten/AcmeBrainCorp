## GOAL C — CoS brief: Against-us / For-our-site / Worth a decision / Proactive

### Against-us (material)
1. **Public price ladders are now table stakes** among Care4Careers, Carrièrepoort, Focus (full 1–5★), CareerAdvisor, Power4People, Xynthesis bands. CH’s proposals-only stance looks opaque in RFPs when buyers Google “outplacement kosten”.
2. **CareerAdvisor** publishes **assessment SKUs from €695** + OP/2e-spoor grids + “AI-vaardigheden” line — closest productized peer.
3. **Carrièrepoort 91%** and **CareerAdvisor 90%** are the only numeric placement peers; both weaker/less specific than CH **>96%/3m**, but CH method notes are not public either → credibility risk if challenged.
4. **Focus €815 entry OP** and **Carrièrepoort from €1.650/€1.950** sit below CH’s informal ~€3k–10k OP band optics.
5. **Leeuwendaal Gallup + MT1000** owns public-sector assessment mindshare without publishing prices (same opacity as CH, stronger brand proof).
6. **Care4Careers kenniscentrum + community** is an SEO/lead machine CH does not match.

### For-our-site
1. CH **>96% within 3 months** remains the strongest timed success claim on the watchlist (peers: 90–91% vague, or none).
2. **COTAN + psych assessments + AcmeBrainCorp agentic AI** still largely unmirrored (CA has LDC + “AI skills” training row only; Leeuwendaal has Gallup not AI).
3. Several strong spoor-2 shops (Xynthesis, P4P, NK, C4C) leave room for CH to stay premium on **assessment-led outplacement** rather than UWV commodity — **HARD LOCK: CH offers no 2e spoor**; peer shops = Against-us only.
4. Opacity peers exist (Leeuwendaal, Nieuwe Koers) — proposals-only is defensible if sales collateral explains bands privately.
