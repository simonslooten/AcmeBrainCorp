## 2) Per competitor — Against-us | For-our-site/sales | concrete win moves

### 2.1 Care4Careers — https://care4careers.nl
**Against-us**
- Massive kenniscentrum “wat kost…” + community lead magnet; outranks CH on price/education SEO.
- Public OP **€2.000–€7.500**/traject; Loopbaanscan **€1.000 excl. btw**; Den Haag spoor 2 **min. €2.300 excl. btw**.
- Stats counters render as **“0 %”** placeholders in static HTML — no verified public % (still owns narrative 3–6 months).

**For-our-site / sales**
- No public success %; no Gallup / CEDEO / OVAL spotted this pass (certs-gap: no own mark claim).
- CH counters with COTAN/psych assessments + **>96%/3m** + AI; Loopbaanscan is vragenlijst + 4 gesprekken — not psycholoog/Gallup brand.
- Cert parity not required vs C4C specifically (neither shows CEDEO/OVAL/Blik as own claim).

**Concrete win moves**
1. Ship “wat kost outplacement / assessment” cornerstone (+ optional OP-vs-2e **differentiatie**: CH doet **geen** 2e spoor) that cites market bands (incl. C4C’s published ranges) + CH resultaat/method — **no CH list price**, **no CH 2e-spoor aanbod**.
2. In proposals: contrast modular €2k–7.5k commodity framing vs assessment-led maatwerk + timed %.
3. Monitor Community (`care4careers.nl/community`) for lead-magnet patterns; do not copy contact tactics that name Simon.
