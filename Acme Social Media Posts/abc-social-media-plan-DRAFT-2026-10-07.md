## 4. Content pillars (tied to the offer)
1. **Waar AI hoort, en waar niet.** The core distinction from the site. *"Als AI niet de juiste stap is, zeggen we dat."*
2. **Besluitkader, geen theater.** The half-day as a decision you can defend, not awareness theatre. EU AI Act in plain language, judging a proposal, what a weak answer looks like (site). **No price.**
3. **Onze vier vragen.** OUR questions, not "a list on the site". One question per post: *Welk probleem lost u werkelijk op? / Is AI het juiste middel? / Wat kost het als dit misgaat? / Hoe ziet goed eruit over 12 maanden?*
4. **Waar zit jij nog in de weg?** The five labels only: *Keep me in · Teach · Systemise · Automate · Escalate only*. Keep me in is a floor, not a failure. (6 Oct draft; that LI draft is EN, so make an NL version for the brand page.)
5. **Bijna-missers (near misses), niet apocalyps.** "The risk is not the tool, it's the gap" (site). Use only cases that are public and sourced. No invented cases and no unsourced stats.
> Rotterdam register: lyrical but sharp, takes the problem seriously but not itself. Hook ≤12 words, e.g. *"Waar zit jij nog in de weg?"* (6 words).

## 5. Traction tactics: 6 reversible tests (organic, no paid boost)
1. **Bottleneck-post A/B across lanes.** NL version on the ABC page (only once page confirmed), EN on Simon personal, 7+ days apart. Measure comments and DMs that ask "how does the hour work?" Not impressions alone.
2. **One four-questions series.** 4 weeks, one question per week on the ABC page (once confirmed), same weekday and slot. Stop after week 2 if no comment or DM shows up.
3. **First-hour seeding (human only).** Simon or a named peer posts 1–2 substantive replies (a disagreement or a case) in the first 60 min. **No agent/bot tip-swarm.**
4. **Line-1 stealable claim.** Open every post with the claim (pattern from post B, AI-not-evil: **48% out-net = personal LI template**, not ABC proof). One lever per post.
5. **Single card on post 1 only.** Chimpanzini makes one card per post. No carousels for 4 weeks, then compare.
6. **Soft ask, 1 in 3 posts — lane-split.**
   - **Simon personal LI:** soft ask without URL — e.g. *"Eerste gesprek? Stuur een DM."* **No acmebraincorp.com.**
   - **ABC company page only:** soft ask without URL-story — same DM line, or *"link in comments / site"* **only if Simon explicitly opens the URL-lock for brand LI**. Default = DM, no company-URL story.
   - X: soft ask rare; no price; no free-call product name.
- Paid boost: **off by default.** Revisit only if organic out-net is ≥~40% *and* comments exist on that template (**personal LI template**, Brr Brr 1 Oct — not ABC proof).
